# Web app surface brief

Scope: the Flippie Talkie web app (`web/`). Visitor mode: Operate. Desk and phone are equal. Keep every feature, the name and the plain wording. The radio, audio and protocol do not change.

Requested visuals: live waveforms, a spectrogram, and an air timeline linked to recordings. Input audio is shown from the moment the microphone is enabled.

Chosen direction: Pulse Sleeve, chosen by the user over the assigned direction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The air is the interface: one white plot of real audio owns the screen, and everything else stands back. Refuses the category default of dark panels around a blue heat-map waterfall.

OWN-WORLD: Matte black ground with no cards or shadows, hairline white rules and square corners. White is the ink. Three coded hues only carry state: blue link, yellow receiving, vermilion transmitting, which appears nowhere else. Archivo at a narrow width, with one very large channel numeral as the only headline.

STORY: The visitor sees what is on the air, tunes it, and holds to talk knowing the channel is open.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with name, state strip, link state and Disconnect. Left: the pulse field fills the height, a spectrogram as stacked white ridge lines, newest at the front, with waveform lanes and the air timeline beneath. Right rail, 368 px: channel numeral, listen, volume, signal and squelch. A fixed talk dock runs along the bottom edge: status, the microphone toggle, and Hold to talk in the bottom-right corner at rail width, carrying the voice trace and the 60-second budget. On phones the field shortens and the dock spans the bottom edge. The talk button keeps one position and size in every state, at the user's request.
