# Code of conduct

This project welcomes anyone who wants to learn, build or help. Be kind, assume good intent, and keep criticism on the work.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination and publishing someone's private information aren't acceptable here. That includes sharing recordings of people who didn't agree to it.

Report a problem through [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or privately to the maintainer. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block people who break these rules.
