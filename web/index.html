    Help

    Browsers

    Use Chrome on Android or a computer. Edge also works over USB. On iPhone and iPad, open this page in Bluefy; Safari can’t connect to Bluetooth devices.

    Connecting

    Keep this page in front while you use the radio. Close the official Flipper mobile app and qFlipper first, because only one app can hold the connection.

    Your other radio

    Select the same channel and turn privacy codes off (code 0). Reception uses the Flipper’s experimental receiver, so some noise is normal.

    Squelch and recording

    Squelch filters by the signal strength the Flipper reports, so strong interference can still pass. Drag it fully left to turn it off. Long transmissions save as consecutive ten-minute parts. Browser data can be cleared, so download clips you care about.

    Talking

    The Flipper sends half a second of silence first so the other radio has time to open. Your opening words are held and sent right after it. Releasing finishes what’s queued; Stop cancels at once.

    Rules

    The Flipper isn’t a certified PMR446 radio. You’re responsible for following local radio rules before you transmit or record.
